Nexora AI Automation: where we train students

Hands-on training in AI and automation, built around real tools.

Nexora Academy of Technology & Innovation trains students to automate real work, not only to talk about it. Our three-month AI and Automation program teaches four tools: Make.com, n8n, Zapier and Airtable. Students learn by building working automations in class, taught by a team of three instructors. Our first cohort is running now, and graduates receive a Certificate of Completion.
